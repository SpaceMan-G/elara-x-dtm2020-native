# Authorization and Licensing Provenance

## Operative publication basis

The repository maintainer received written clarification from Sean Bruinsma concerning publication of a DTM2020 implementation.

The clarification, retained privately by the maintainer, established the following operative points for this publication:

1. Code written from the scientific publications may be made publicly available.
2. The publications alone do not expose the full coefficient-variation mapping needed for an exact implementation.
3. Using the official subroutine as necessary to understand/convert that operational structure is acceptable.
4. Distribution of the model coefficients is acceptable provided the coefficients are distributed with the applicable licence.
5. Scientific results may be made public, subject to responsible presentation and the licence conditions.

On that basis this repository:

- publishes the independently structured Python implementation;
- includes `data/DTM_2020_F107_Kp.dat`;
- includes the applicable official licence as `LICENSE`;
- does **not** redistribute the original SWAMI/CNES Fortran source;
- provides explicit CNES/SWAMI attribution and provenance;
- presents validation results neutrally.

The correspondence itself is not included in the public repository.

## Published licence text

The licence bundled here is copied from the official SWAMI MCM source used for the accepted Elara X DTM2020 provenance baseline. Users must read and comply with that licence.

## Separation of materials

The presence of Elara X-authored Python files beside the DTM coefficient resource does not remove or weaken CNES ownership or licence conditions associated with DTM2020 and its supplied model data.
